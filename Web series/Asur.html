Asur: Welcome to Your Dark Side

IMDb Rating: ⭐ 8.5/10

Genre: Crime | Drama | Mystery | Thriller

Creator: Gaurav Shukla

Stars: Arshad Warsi, Barun Sobti, Ridhi Dogra, Anupriya Goenka, Sharib Hashmi

Duration: 2 Seasons | 16 Episodes | Approx. 50m per episode

Release Year: 2020 - 2023

Story: Nikhil Nair, a forensic expert turned teacher, is forced to return to the CBI to catch a brutal serial killer who believes himself to be the Asur - the demon from Indian mythology. With the help of his former colleague Dhananjay Rajpoot, Nikhil must solve a series of chilling murders linked to ancient scriptures, religion, and technology. As the killer gets closer, Nikhil must face his own dark past.
