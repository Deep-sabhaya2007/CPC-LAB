Squid Game

IMDb Rating: ⭐ 8.0/10

Genre: Drama | Mystery | Thriller

Creator: Hwang Dong-hyuk

Stars: Lee Jung-jae, Jung Ho-yeon, Park Hae-soo, Lee Byung-hun, Wi Ha-joon

Duration: 3 Seasons | 22 Episodes | Approx. 60m per episode

Release Year: 2021 - 2025

Story: Hundreds of cash-strapped players accept a strange invitation to compete in children's games for a tempting prize of 45.6 billion won. But the games turn deadly, and losing means death. Seong Gi-hun, a desperate divorced father, must survive brutal games like Red Light Green Light and the Squid Game itself, while forming alliances and facing betrayal. In the end, only one player can walk away alive.
