Duration: 1 Season | 10 Episodes | Approx. 50m per episode

Story: Based on the true story of Harshad Mehta, a small-town stockbroker who rises to become the biggest name in the Bombay Stock Exchange. Using loopholes in the banking system, he builds a massive empire and becomes known as the Big Bull. But his greed and risky game soon attract journalists, bankers, and the authorities, leading to one of the biggest financial scams in Indian history.
