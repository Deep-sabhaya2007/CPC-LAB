Panchayat

IMDb Rating: ⭐ 8.9/10

Genre: Comedy | Drama

Director: Deepak Kumar Mishra

Stars: Jitendra Kumar, Neena Gupta, Raghubir Yadav, Faisal Malik, Chandan Roy, Sanvikaa

Duration: 4 Seasons | 32 Episodes | Approx. 35m per episode

Release Year: 2020 - 2025

Story: Abhishek Tripathi, an engineering graduate, reluctantly takes a job as the secretary of the Panchayat office in the small village of Phulera in Uttar Pradesh. Far from city life, he faces funny village politics, strange office problems, and unforgettable people like Pradhan Ji, Manju Devi, Vikas, and Prahlad. While dreaming of a better job and preparing for his CAT exam, Abhishek slowly starts understanding and loving village life.
